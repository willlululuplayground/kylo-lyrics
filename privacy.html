Kylo Lyrics – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

English简体中文繁體中文

English

Kylo Lyrics ("the app") shows time-synced lyrics for music you play in Spotify. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking. The app is free and has no in-app purchases.

Information stored only on your device

  • The Spotify Client ID you enter, and the sign-in tokens Spotify gives the app, are stored on your device (tokens in the iOS Keychain). They are used only to talk to Spotify on your behalf.
  • Downloaded lyrics, your saved playlists list and app settings are stored on your device so the app works offline.

Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Services the app connects to

  • Spotify (Spotify Web API and the Spotify app on your device): to read your playlists and what is playing, and to control playback. Your use of Spotify is covered by Spotify's privacy policy.
  • LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a public lyrics database: to find lyrics, the app sends the song title, artist, album and duration. No account or personal information is sent.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. For questions, please open an issue at github.com/willlululuplayground/kylo-lyrics/issues.

简体中文

Kylo Lyrics（下称“本 App”）为你在 Spotify 中播放的音乐显示同步歌词。本政策说明本 App 会处理哪些信息。简而言之：我们不收集、不在服务器上保存、不出售、不分享任何个人数据。本 App 没有统计分析、广告或追踪。本 App 完全免费，没有任何 App 内购买。

只保存在你设备上的信息

  • 你填写的 Spotify Client ID，以及 Spotify 给本 App 的登录凭证，只保存在你的设备上（凭证保存在 iOS 钥匙串中），仅用于代表你和 Spotify 通信。
  • 下载的歌词、歌单列表和 App 设置保存在你的设备上，以便离线使用。

删除本 App 即会从设备上删除这些数据。

本 App 连接的服务

  • Spotify（Spotify 网络接口及你设备上的 Spotify App）：读取你的歌单和正在播放的内容，并控制播放。你对 Spotify 的使用受 Spotify 隐私政策约束。
  • LRCLIB（lrclib.net，公开的歌词数据库）：查找歌词时，本 App 会发送歌名、歌手、专辑和时长，不发送任何账号或个人信息。

儿童

本 App 不面向儿童，也不会有意收集任何人的信息。

变更与联系
